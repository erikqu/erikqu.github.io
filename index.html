Background

Before Refresh, I was lead MLE at LynkAI, working on large-scale voice data and models, and an early engineer at Superdial, working on IVR navigation and evals around medical phone calls using GPT 3.5 Turbo.

Earlier, I worked on large-scale data at Capital One and scraping at Amazon, where I submitted a patent in 2021 on automatically generating scrapers.

My research focused on GAN-based user preference modeling and cross-view synthesis (ACM-MM ’20, ECCV ’20).

College projects & competitions

In college, I worked on financial modeling through the Citadel Aperture program and competed on Numerai (2× Gold, 99th percentile; 5× Silver in Numerai Fund’s Weekly AI/ML Competition). I also worked on adversarial synthetic data generation extensively for the Numerai competition.
